# User Stories

## Story 1: Account Creation
- **As a** customer,
- **I want** to create a new account in the service,
- **So that** I can manage my financial details.

## Story 2: Read Account
- **As a** customer,
- **I want** to read my account details,
- **So that** I can review my information.

## Story 3: Update Account
- **As a** customer,
- **I want** to update my account details,
- **So that** I can keep my information current.

## Story 4: Delete Account
- **As a** customer,
- **I want** to delete my account,
- **So that** my data is removed when no longer needed.
